Scoped audit

A fixed-scope look at one system — the stack, the data, or the process — ending in a document you can hand to whoever does the work.

  • Fixed scope and fixed price, both agreed before I start
  • One week from kickoff to document
  • Root-cause analysis, not a symptom list
  • Findings ranked by what actually costs you money
  • Concrete remediation steps, sized
  • Yours to execute, with or without me

You pay when the audit lands. Not up front, not on kickoff. If what I hand you isn't worth it, say so and we're done.
